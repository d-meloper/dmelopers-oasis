# Asset Pipeline

Read the applicable [asset experience](references/best-practices.md) before choosing an approach.
Use it for topology-aware weights, stale packaging and consumer checks.

Inspect or author the requested asset through its owning tool or generator,
preserving editable originals and the consumer's rig, axes, textures and motion.
Validate the changed deformation and packaged resource rather than relying on a
bind-pose preview.

Before the final completion report, consolidate useful lessons from this work
once in the owning experience reference.
