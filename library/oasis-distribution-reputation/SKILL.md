# Distribution Reputation

Identify the reported download or site and distinguish reputation, detection,
signature and byte-mismatch causes. Prepare the factual vendor report for the
requested target and verify its case outcome.
Use [reputation experience](references/best-practices.md) for exact released
identity, warning context and uncertain submission recovery.
