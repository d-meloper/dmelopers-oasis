# Knowledge lifecycle

When work reveals a reusable experience, update the nearest role's
`references/best-practices.md` or an existing suitable guide. Explain enough of
the conditions, problem, useful method and evidence to make it applicable.
Distinguish observed behavior from a hypothesis and fixtures from installed or
live behavior. There is no fixed prose form, mandatory retrospective or evidence
field checklist.

Merge repeated advice around one source and link from other roles. Update stale
advice when its assumptions change; retain unique historical evidence with a
replacement link. An empty placeholder or generic caution is not new experience.

Keep project knowledge and settings local. Put raw transcripts, private paths,
account/chat IDs and operational evidence in `.oasis/local/`; portable lessons
use sanitized examples. Local experience does not automatically propagate to
upstream or other projects. Use the existing knowledge commands when a structured
case or a requested sanitized handoff is useful; ordinary reference edits do not
require that format. See the [knowledge manual](../.oasis/manual/knowledge.md).
